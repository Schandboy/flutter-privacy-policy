1 Introduction

Susan Chikanbanjar ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ludo mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2 Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Email address (when you create an account)
  • Display name or profile name (if provided)
  • Profile photo (if uploaded)

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • App usage data and interaction events
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not GPS)

Game data:

  • Game statistics (wins, losses, games played, tokens captured)
  • Match history and player rankings
  • Achievement progress
  • Purchase status (premium/ad-free)

Multiplayer data:

  • Online match session data
  • Player identifiers used during multiplayer sessions
  • In-game chat messages (if applicable)

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Purpose Data Used
Account creation & authentication Email, authentication tokens
Providing the game experience Game stats, achievements, match history
Online multiplayer matchmaking Player ID, ranking data, session info
Displaying advertisements Advertising ID, device info, approximate location
Processing in-app purchases Purchase tokens (handled by Google Play / App Store)

4 Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication — for user account management. Privacy Policy
  • Cloud Firestore — for storing game data, player profiles, and match history. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — for serving advertisements. Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — for processing in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — for crash reporting and diagnostics. Privacy Policy

These services may collect information used to identify you. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5 Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID and device information to serve personalized ads.

You can control ad personalization:

  • On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of personalized advertising
  • On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to manage app tracking
  • Purchase the ad-free version through our in-app purchase option to remove all ads entirely

Premium users who have purchased ad-free access will not be shown any advertisements, and no ad-related data will be collected for them.

6 In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements. All purchase transactions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS). We do not collect, process, or store any payment information such as credit card numbers.

We only receive a purchase confirmation token from the respective platform to verify your premium status and remove ads from your experience.

7 Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate measures to protect it:

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • User authentication is handled securely through Firebase Authentication
  • Database access is protected by Firestore Security Rules restricting access to authorized users only
  • Multiplayer session data is transmitted through secure channels

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Children's Privacy

The App is rated for general audiences but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

9 Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services.

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account information (name, email, profile photo)
  • Achievement progress and game statistics
  • Match history and ranking data
  • All Firestore documents associated with your user ID

You can delete your account by:

  • In-app: Tap the profile icon → Delete Account → Confirm
  • Web: Visit our Account Deletion page
  • Email: Contact us at scbtechsolution@gmail.com

Account deletion is processed within 30 days of the request. Anonymized, aggregated data that cannot be linked to your identity may be retained for analytics purposes.

10 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out — opt out of personalized advertising

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below.
