Ludo — Account & Data Deletion

What data will be deleted?

When you request account deletion, the following data associated with your account will be permanently removed:

  • Your email address and authentication credentials
  • Game history, match records, and statistics
  • Player rankings and achievement progress
  • In-app purchase records (you will lose premium/ad-free status)
  • Any preferences or settings tied to your account

⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. If you purchased ad-free access, you will need to purchase it again with a new account.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account using either method:

Option 1: In-App

Open the Ludo app
Go to Settings → Account
Tap "Delete Account"
Confirm deletion when prompted

Option 2: Submit a request below

If you cannot access the app, use the form below.

Request Account Deletion

We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data Retention Policy

After account deletion is processed:

  • All personal data is permanently deleted within 7 business days
  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics data may be retained (not linked to your identity)
  • Data required by law may be retained for the legally mandated period
