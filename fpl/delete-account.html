FPL App

Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your FPL App account and what happens to your data.

01 How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the app by following these steps:

  1. Open the FPL App on your device
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon)
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at scbtechsolution@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" from the email address associated with your account.

02 What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days:

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your email address and display name
  • Your Firebase Authentication credentials
  • Your saved FPL Manager ID and team preferences
  • Your notification preferences and FCM token
  • Any personalization data tied to your account

Data That May Be Retained

  • Aggregated analytics — anonymized, non-identifiable usage statistics may be retained for app improvement purposes
  • Crash logs — anonymized diagnostic data may be retained for up to 90 days
  • Legal obligations — data required to be retained by law will be kept for the legally required period

03 Important Things to Know

This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. You will need to create a new account if you wish to use the app again.
  • If you purchased ad-free access via Google Play, your purchase is managed by Google and is not affected by account deletion. You can manage your subscriptions through the Google Play Store.
  • Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account. You must follow the steps above to delete your account and associated data.
  • Account deletion requests made via email will be processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
